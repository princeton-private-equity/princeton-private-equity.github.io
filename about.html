About Us

Founded in 2023, Princeton Private Equity educates Princeton undergraduates about investing fundamentals, with a strong emphasis on private equity and the growing landscape of buy-side opportunities.

Our club brings together students interested in understanding businesses, evaluating investments, and exploring careers in finance. We combine a foundation in financial analysis with practical learning and a community of peers, alumni, and industry professionals.

Education

Our education program teaches finance and investing fundamentals from the ground up. We welcome students with no prior financial knowledge and introduce the accounting, valuation, and analytical tools needed to understand how investors assess a business.

Members apply these concepts through take-private case studies and leveraged buyout (LBO) modeling. By examining how a transaction is financed and how an investment can create value, students develop a practical understanding of private equity.

Recruiting & Professional Development

We prepare members for careers in finance through technical instruction, interview preparation, and mentorship from students who have been through the recruiting process. By building both practical skills and an understanding of industry opportunities, members approach recruiting with confidence.

Community & Alumni

Learning continues through industry presentations and connections with our alumni network and sponsors. These relationships give members opportunities to learn from experienced professionals and explore career paths. We aim to build a supportive community where students can exchange ideas and stay connected beyond their time at Princeton.
