About Us

Founded in 2023, Princeton Private Equity educates Princeton undergraduates about investing fundamentals, with a strong emphasis on private equity and the growing landscape of buy-side opportunities.

Our education program teaches finance and investing fundamentals from the ground up, with no prior experience required. Members apply what they learn through take-private case studies and leveraged buyout (LBO) modeling.

We prepare members for careers in finance through technical instruction, interview preparation, and mentorship, while connecting them with our alumni network and sponsors to build lasting professional relationships.
